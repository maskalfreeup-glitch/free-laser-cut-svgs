Shop Compatibility & Specs

Every file in this set is designed for the following machines, software, and material tolerances — verify calibration on your own hardware before production runs.

Supported Machines

  • Glowforge Basic, Plus, Pro
  • xTool D1, M1, P2
  • OMTech CO2 series
  • Creality Falcon diode series
  • Shapeoko / Carbide CNC routers

Software Verified

  • LightBurn v1.2+
  • LaserGRBL
  • Inkscape
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Silhouette Studio Business Edition

Kerf Tolerance (Design Target)

  • Designed for 0.08mm–0.18mm beam spot diameters
  • 3mm (1/8") Birch Plywood
  • Cast Acrylic sheet stock
  • MDF standard density
